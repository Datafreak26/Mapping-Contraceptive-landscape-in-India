# **Title**<br>
#### Mapping India's Contraceptive Landscape<br>
---
# **Overview**<br>
#### This project aims to explore India's contraceptive landscape through exploratory data analysis (EDA), focusing on regional variations and demographic patterns in contraceptive use. The objective is to identify meaningful trends and disparities in the data and develop insights that contribute to a better understanding of contraceptive practices across India.<br>
---
# **Business Problem**<br>
#### A pharmaceutical company planning to enter India's contraceptive market needs to understand regional differences in contraceptive use and preferences to identify potential market opportunities.  The company needs to understand state-level differences  , the distribution of methods used, and variations across demographic groups to prioritize target markets and product opportunities.<br>

# **Key Questions**<br>
Q1. Which type of contraceptive method is most commonly used across different regions?
